Assumptions to know

  • The librosa key and pitch detectors are annotated O(s) in decoded samples. That relies on librosa's fixed STFT/CQT window sizes (n_fft is capped at MAX_FFT), which is library-internal and not visible in this repo.
  • The ffmpeg calls are bounded by what Python does: bytes captured from the child process, and samples the child decodes.
  • bank.pcm returns a defensive copy, so BankValidator._check_samples and the pcm_used fallbacks copy up to 24 MiB just to read a length. That is linear and allowed, but avoidable.

Bug found during the audit (not fixed)

Opening a Save-PSG file crashes the Pattern tab.

BankSession.open → read_file → read_psg builds a Bank with no patterns. PatternView.bind_bank then calls bank.blank_pattern_bytes(), whose max() over an empty dict raises ValueError: max() iterable argument is empty. This was reproduced directly. The fix needs a guard for empty pattern lists in blank_pattern_bytes, or in its callers. It is left for a separate fix/ branch.
